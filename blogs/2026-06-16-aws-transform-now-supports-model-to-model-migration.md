AWS Transform offers a model-to-model migration custom transformation that assesses generative AI workloads and produces plans for moving from third-party providers to Amazon Bedrock. The AI-powered agent scans codebases, identifies AI SDKs and models, and maps Bedrock equivalents with cost comparisons, supporting migrations from OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, and open-source models via LiteLLM or Ollama.
